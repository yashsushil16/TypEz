Privacy Model

Zero-Telemetry By Design

Traditional text assistants harvest keystrokes on remote servers. TypEz is architected to keep your personal data strictly confined to your device.

01

Direct Client-to-API Calls

When you invoke the AI engine, requests travel directly from the background service worker to Groq or Google's endpoints. No middleman server inspects or logs your text.

02

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Use your personal free-tier API keys from Groq Cloud and Google AI Studio. Your keys are stored exclusively in Chrome's encrypted storage sandbox on your computer.

03

Volatile Keystroke Buffer

TypEz monitors memory only after an explicit trigger symbol (? or !) is typed. Normal typing is never buffered, stored, or analyzed.
