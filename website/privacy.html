Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 • Strict Zero-Telemetry Standard

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy

TypEz was engineered with one fundamental rule: Your keystrokes and personal text belong exclusively to you. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not log keystrokes, we do not employ analytics SDKs, and we never sell user data.

2. Information We Handle and Where It Lives

TypEz handles three specific categories of data:

  • Text Expansion Shortcuts: The phrases, symbols, and expansion bodies you create (such as ?@ for your email) are stored exclusively in your browser's local sandbox via chrome.storage.local. They never leave your device unless you explicitly configure the optional self-hosted sync server.
  • API Keys: When you provide your personal Groq or Google Gemini API keys in the settings popup, they are stored directly in your local extension storage. They are only transmitted directly to the respective API providers (Groq Cloud or Google Generative Language) to execute your explicit requests.
  • Keystroke Buffer: The content script monitors keystrokes in volatile memory only after a designated trigger symbol (? or !) is pressed. The buffer is completely cleared as soon as you press Space, Enter, or Escape, or if you backspace to empty. Normal keystrokes are never buffered, persisted, or logged.

3. Third-Party Language Model Providers

When you use the AI transformation engine by typing an ! command, TypEz captures the preceding paragraph of text and sends it directly from your browser's background service worker to:

  • Groq Cloud API: Located at api.groq.com (subject to Groq's privacy policy).
  • Google Gemini API: Located at generativelanguage.googleapis.com (subject to Google's API service terms).

No other party receives this transmission. TypEz does not route prompts through any proxy or telemetry layer.

4. Permissions Used by TypEz

Under Google Chrome Manifest V3, TypEz requests only the permissions necessary for its dual engines:

  • storage: Required to persist your snippets, prompt templates, and API keys locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • activeTab: Required to access the active DOM input when you focus on a field.
  • <all_urls>: Required so text expansion works seamlessly across all web applications (Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, Slack, Twitter/X, and custom CMS fields).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain 100% control over all data. You can inspect, modify, or delete any shortcut or API key at any time through the extension popup. If you uninstall TypEz from Google Chrome, all local settings, snippets, and cached data are instantly purged by the browser.

6. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because TypEz does not collect, store, or process personal data on external centralized servers, we do not maintain tracking profiles or sell consumer data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
